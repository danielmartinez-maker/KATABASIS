For each route, use the named foundation and its ability loop: Cast routes place original impacts or fan projectiles; Guard routes time fresh parries; Dash routes sequence movement into offense; critical routes use quick direct attacks; damage-over-time routes apply their supplied statuses; shield routes protect and replenish the supplied ward; kill routes maintain enemy chains. Ordinary generic gear supplies level-capped offense, defense or recovery, and any other god can supply a complementary loop. No specific deity or highest-rarity item is mandatory.

Repeated ordinary or Rare boon offers, run augments, Fated Threads, and level-30 generic gear provide an endgame path. Mythic traits and Godforged imprints can transform these routes but are optional. All 18 mechanic riders retain the shared trigger caps and every compiled character uses the same finite stat ceiling.

Verification: tools/endgame-overhaul.test.js drafts every foundation through the real choice engine, equips no highest-rarity item or branch prerequisite, activates its real combat trigger, and defeats a bounded hostile using ordinary build damage. This covers 96 routes. These deterministic behavioral fixtures demonstrate reachability and functioning mechanics; they are not player win-rate or difficulty telemetry. Further balance tuning should compare full-run clear rates and time-to-kill across representative modifier combinations.
