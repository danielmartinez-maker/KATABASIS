HOW TO DESCEND

Controls

  • W A S D — move
  • Mouse — aim
  • Left Click / hold — Strike (3-hit combo)
  • Right Click / hold — Guard (block, reflect, parry)
  • F — Cast (hurled spear, leaves a rift)
  • Space — Dash (charges, brief invulnerability)
  • R — Rush (long invulnerable charge)
  • K — Wrath (radial nova)
  • Q — Call (a god answers)
  • T — Ascend (brief godhood)
  • E — interact / take / pass a gate
  • Story — Enter / Space / → advance; ESC skip to choice
  • ESC — pause  ·  M — mute
  • Tab — moves keyboard focus (never remapped); on touch, use the on-screen DASH / GUARD / CAST / TAKE buttons. Touch aims with one finger; movement needs a keyboard.

Your Eight Answers

Strike is your damage. Guard is your defence — raise it and blows from the front are largely stopped, and any projectile that touches it is reflected. Raise it at the instant of impact and you parry: no damage at all, a stagger, and your next blows land 150% harder.

Cast gives you reach — a thrown spear that bursts into a lingering rift. Dash is your dodge, on charges. Rush is a long invulnerable charge that scatters a crowd. Ascend makes you untouchable, faster and stronger for five seconds — save it for when the chamber turns.

The Descent

The campaign crosses twenty-four regions with eight chambers each, grouped into three eight-region acts. Choose routes through combat, blood-tithe bargains, mythic nemeses, champions, treasure, Charon's shop, trials, fateful events, and optional bosses. Search each arena for image-carved relic props, and choose whether to haggle or trade life for coin at Charon's. After every capstone, speak with the gods and heroes; your answers shape the final covenant. Every enemy you slay is remembered in the Chronicle, and a foe that ends a run may return as your Nemesis.

Boons

The Olympians offer their power between chambers. Rarer boons do more. Duos and Legendaries require the right foundation. The effect lines show the exact values at the offered rarity. Your build after choosing shows the resulting changes after stacking and caps. Upgrades show the old value, new value, and difference.

Death Defiance

Certain boons and relics let you survive a killing blow. You will need them.
